About Me

Hi! I'm Haocheng, a PhD student at Tohoku University and a member of the Fundamental Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (FaiLab) . My research focuses on natural language processing and large language models, particularly data selection, post-training, and making model training more efficient.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing PC games, getting some work done at a cozy café, or recharging at the gym or through basketball and football. I learned the violin when I was little, and I still enjoy trying out different musical instruments and listening to classical music and relaxing instrumentals.
